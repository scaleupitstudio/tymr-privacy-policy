Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-11-23

Tymr: Pomodoro & Tasks (“the App”) is a productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, track focus sessions, and improve time management. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

Information We Collect

Tymr does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive data.

  • We do not collect names, emails, or contact information.
  • We do not collect device identifiers.
  • We do not track user behavior.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not send any information to external servers.

Local Device Storage

The App may store the following data locally on your device only, solely for your convenience:

  • Tasks you create
  • Pomodoro session data
  • Timer settings
  • Theme settings (light/dark mode)
  • Event reminders
  • Productivity statistics

None of this data leaves your device unless you choose to export or sync it using a feature that explicitly sends data (not currently available).

Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or store any personal data from children.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or use analytics services, advertising networks, user-tracking SDKs, cloud storage services, or authentication services.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally and can be deleted by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. After uninstalling, all app data is permanently removed from your device.

Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored externally, the risk of unauthorized access is extremely low. However, please ensure your device is secure to protect locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: developerscaleupstudio@gmail.com.
